How it works

  1. Choose a text-based curriculum PDF, up to 30 MB.
  2. Optionally enter the pages to use, such as 3-5, and pick your ICSE standard.
  3. Select Play Detective or Play Superhero to turn the facts into double-sided game cards.
  4. Preview the cards, then create a lesson PDF to print or share.

Questions teachers ask

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. You can use the tool without signing up, and there is no paid AI call.

What kind of PDF works best?

Text-based PDFs work best. Scanned pages may need OCR first.

Should I check the cards before teaching?

Yes. Review any flagged claims against your textbook before using the cards in class.
