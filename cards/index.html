On paper

A4, 10 cards a page, business-card size. Print fronts and backs double-sided ("flip on long edge"), then cut along the dashed lines.

Cards use only the words in your PDF. Every card back is checked word-for-word against the sheet before it can be printed. Pictures are icons from Tabler Icons (MIT licence); "draw it!" means none fitted.
